Hips

The tape wraps around the fullest part of your hips, level all the way around.

  1. Step 1

    Stand with your feet together and weight even on both feet.

  2. Step 2

    Find the fullest part of your hips and rear — usually about 20cm (8in) below your waist.

  3. Step 3

    Wrap the tape around that point, keeping it parallel to the floor all the way around. Check in a mirror, or ask someone to check the back for you.

  4. Step 4

    Pull the tape snug against your body — not tight enough to squeeze or leave a mark.

  5. Step 5

    Read the number where the tape meets the zero end.
