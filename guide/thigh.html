Thigh

The tape wraps around the fullest part of your upper thigh, level all the way around.

  1. Step 1

    Stand with your weight even on both feet.

  2. Step 2

    Find the fullest part of your thigh — usually near the top, just below your hip.

  3. Step 3

    Wrap the tape around that point, keeping it level all the way around.

  4. Step 4

    Pull the tape snug against your skin — not tight enough to squeeze or leave a mark.

  5. Step 5

    Read the number where the tape meets the zero end.
