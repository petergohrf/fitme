Waist

The tape wraps around your natural waist, level all the way around.

  1. Step 1

    Stand naturally, relaxed — don't hold your stomach in or push it out.

  2. Step 2

    Find your natural waist — usually the narrowest point above your belly button and below your ribs. If you're not sure, bend gently to one side; the crease that forms is your waist.

  3. Step 3

    Wrap the tape around that point, keeping it parallel to the floor all the way around.

  4. Step 4

    Pull the tape snug against your body — not tight enough to squeeze or leave a mark.

  5. Step 5

    Breathe out normally and read the number where the tape meets the zero end.
