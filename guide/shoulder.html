Shoulder width

The tape runs straight across your back, from the edge of one shoulder to the edge of the other.

  1. Step 1

    Stand naturally with your arms relaxed at your sides.

  2. Step 2

    This one's tricky to do alone — ask someone to help if you can.

  3. Step 3

    Measure straight across your back, from the edge of one shoulder (where your arm meets your shoulder) to the edge of the other.

  4. Step 4

    Keep the tape flat and straight — don't let it follow the curve of your neck or back.

  5. Step 5

    Read the number where the tape reaches the far shoulder.
